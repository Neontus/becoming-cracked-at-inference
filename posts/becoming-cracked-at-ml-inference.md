This page will document how I’m going to hopefully remedy both of these.

Along the way, some of my goals are to show consistent, disciplined effort / interest in this field, to be able to explain what I’m doing to people at multiple levels of understanding (complete beginners → ML researchers), and to have a cool project (or a few) that I can show off as my very own.
